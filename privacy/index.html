Website privacy policy.

This policy explains how Sari3 LLC handles information when you visit sari3.io or contact us directly.

Information this website collects

Sari3 LLC does not use analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, user accounts, or contact forms on this website. The pages are static and do not ask you to submit personal information.

Hosting and technical requests

Like most websites, the infrastructure that delivers sari3.io may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and time of access for security, reliability, and delivery. Sari3 LLC does not receive a visitor analytics dashboard from this site or combine this information to profile visitors.

When you contact us

If you email or call Sari3 LLC, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, and message. We use it to respond, provide support, maintain business records, protect our services, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing and sale

Sari3 LLC does not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate, when required by law, or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of our services.

Retention and choices

Business communications are kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose they were received, support history, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent directly to us, subject to applicable exceptions.

Product-specific policies

Individual Sari3 products may have different data practices. Tawba’s practices are described in the Tawba Privacy Policy.

Contact
