Before contacting us

Include your device model, operating-system version, Tawba version, what you expected, what happened, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive worship records.

Local data

Tawba stores plans and prayer logs only on your device. Sari3 LLC cannot view or restore those records. Never email sensitive worship data unless you intentionally choose to share it.

Manage your data

Open Tawba Settings to export your data, adjust an estimate, disable reminders, or permanently reset local records. See Your Privacy Choices.

Religious questions

Tawba is a planning tool, not a source of authoritative religious guidance. Consult a qualified scholar for rulings specific to your circumstances.
