Before contacting us

Include your device model, operating-system version, Tawba version, what you expected, what happened, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive worship records.

Local data and recovery

Tawba stores plans and prayer logs on your device; Sari3 LLC has no server copy and cannot remotely recover deleted records. A supported JSON copy you previously exported can be restored locally through Restore from backup in Settings. Review the summary before confirming: restore replaces current records rather than merging them. Re-enable optional reminders and the prayer countdown afterward.

Keep a private export before reinstalling or resetting the app. Exports are not encrypted by Tawba. Do not email a backup unless you intentionally choose to disclose its contents.

Manage your data

Open Tawba Settings to export or restore a copy, adjust your estimate, disable reminders, remove the saved countdown location, or reset local records. If saving, reminder cancellation, or reset reports an error, follow the displayed instructions and retry; do not assume an incomplete action succeeded. See Your Privacy Choices.

Religious questions

Tawba is a planning tool, not a source of authoritative religious guidance. New setup accepts an estimate you choose or lets you track without a total. It does not automatically calculate religious obligations. Consult a qualified scholar for rulings specific to your circumstances.
