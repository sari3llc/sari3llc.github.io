Before contacting support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Tawba version and build (shown in Settings), what you expected, what happened and the steps to reproduce it. Redact personal details in screenshots. Do not send passwords, payment details or prayer-history files. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step before saving a usable backup outside Tawba.

Why did my remaining total change?

Daily prayers, Make-ups and Additional prayers are separate records. Unlogged prayers stay unrecorded after midnight, time away and restoration. Marking a prayer Missed lets you choose whether to add it to the estimate or link it to an already included count. Make-ups reduce the matching remaining count; additional prayers do not settle missed daily prayers. Earlier versions may have added automatic missed records or filled prayers as Prayed. Those existing records and balances are preserved until you correct them in the calendar; automatic counting and auto-fill no longer run.

How do I mark days Not applicable?

Open Daily on the dashboard or calendar, then choose Not applicable dates. Select one day or an inclusive range of up to 366 days, ending today or earlier. Preview shows how many unrecorded or missed prayers will change, which recorded prayers stay unchanged, and any reduction to your five balances. Prayed records, completed make-ups and existing Not applicable entries are preserved. Added or automatically counted missed entries are removed from their matching balance; removing an already-included link does not reduce your starting estimate. Confirm only after reviewing the preview. If a record or balance changes before saving, preview again. You do not need to give a reason. To correct a completed or prayed record, use its individual Edit control instead.

What do the five daily counts show?

Daily prayers remaining shows the exact number of recorded Missed prayers that have not been linked to a completed make-up, separately for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha. A missed prayer appears immediately, even before you decide how to include it in the saved estimate. Prayed, Not applicable, made-up and unrecorded prayers do not count. Zero means no outstanding missed records for that prayer; it does not estimate unrecorded history. Earlier automatic missed records remain counted until you correct them.

The Saved remaining estimate is separate and may already include these daily misses. Do not add the two figures together. Use a Daily record's Add to estimate control to say whether it is already included or should add one. A make-up linked to that record removes it from the daily count; undo brings it back. A make-up against older history alone changes only the saved estimate.

How do I edit the saved estimate?

Tap Set breakdown in the dashboard's Saved remaining estimate section, or Set prayer breakdown in Settings. Older versions saved one total, so Tawba keeps that total intact and shows the part still unassigned. Enter all five estimates, or use Split unassigned evenly to prepare a draft and edit it before saving. The five estimates must cover their linked missed records and add up to the saved total. Assigning an estimate does not change the exact daily counts.

After assignment, Edit counts lets you revise each estimate. A Fajr make-up reduces the Fajr estimate only; undo restores the same prayer. Saving is blocked if another log or correction changes the breakdown while you are editing; load the latest counts and review again.

How do prayer alerts work?

Set your prayer-time location and calculation method in Settings, then enable Daily prayer notifications, Not logged yet, or both. New alerts say Tap to mark as prayed. Tap only when you intend to record that prayer; receiving or dismissing an alert does not log it. Existing records, including Not applicable, are not overwritten.

Alerts are scheduled up to five days ahead. Reopen Tawba to refresh them, check iPhone notification/Focus settings, and refresh location after travel. Fajr warnings use sunrise; Dhuhr, Asr and Maghrib warnings use the next calculated prayer. Isha's one-hour check-in is not a religious deadline. The separate make-up reminder can be turned off independently.

Why do prayer times or the compass differ?

Choose the calculation method and Standard or Hanafi Asr setting used by your local community. Refresh location after travel. Prayer times are estimates, not a substitute for local guidance. Start compass below the calendar uses temporary location and heading readings for approximate Qibla direction. Poor sensor readings can hide the arrow. Move away from magnetic interference and follow the app's instructions. Denied location permission does not prevent prayer logging. Stop the compass when finished.

How do I keep or restore my records?

Settings > Export backup opens the share sheet. Save the unencrypted file somewhere private outside Tawba. There is no automatic cloud backup or cross-device sync; do not assume iPhone or iCloud backups include prayer history. The developer cannot restore a lost local database.

Settings > Restore backup accepts a Tawba file or pasted JSON. Review the records and totals in the preview. Restoration replaces records instead of merging them and has no general undo. Export your current records first. New backups preserve the prayer breakdown and records. Older formats remain supported without guessing a historical prayer distribution. Unlogged days stay unrecorded, and old automatic-counting or auto-fill preferences are not re-enabled. Update Tawba before importing a new-format backup. Restored notifications, reminders and saved location stay off until you enable them again.

Can I delete data or stop location access?

Settings > Delete all data asks before removing local records and settings and canceling reminders and prayer alerts. Delete recovery copy removes only an original retained during damaged-data recovery. Remove location erases saved prayer-time coordinates and time zone. Revoking iPhone permission alone does not erase a previously saved location. Exported files and Apple's purchase history must be managed separately.

Are tips required?

No. Where Apple products are available, Support Tawba > Leave a tip opens optional one-time purchases. Tips unlock no features and are not subscriptions or charitable fundraising. Apple handles payment confirmation, purchase history and refund requests. A pending approval is not a completed tip. If a result is uncertain, check Apple purchase history before trying again; do not repeatedly retry payment. TestFlight purchases use Apple's test environment rather than charging testers.

Apple purchase help: https://support.apple.com/billing

Religious questions

Tawba is a record-keeping tool, not a source of authoritative religious rulings. Starting estimates and recorded statuses are user-controlled. Consult a qualified scholar about obligations, exemptions and personal circumstances.
