Before contacting support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Tawba version/build from Settings, expected behavior and steps to reproduce. Redact personal records from screenshots. Do not send passwords, payment details or prayer-history files. Save a usable backup before deleting the app.

What do the five remaining counts mean?

They count recorded Daily misses without a completed make-up link, including misses not assigned to the saved estimate. Tap a prayer to review the dates. The saved remaining estimate is separate and can overlap; do not add the two sections. Unlogged prayers stay unrecorded after midnight, time away and restoration.

How do I complete a recorded miss?

Open it from its count or the calendar. You can complete a miss without adding it to a historical estimate, or link an eligible make-up you already logged. Review whether the saved estimate changes. A completion linked to that estimate reduces the matching prayer; an independent Daily completion leaves the saved remaining estimate unchanged. Additional prayers do not settle Daily misses or the estimate.

Can I record several prayers at once?

Use a typed make-up batch: choose the prayer, quantity, date and whether to reduce the estimate. Preview its effect before saving. Undo the batch after reviewing which links and counts will reopen. Whole-day Prayed fills only the unrecorded prayers you select. Existing records require their own explicit corrections.

How do I review older records?

Open History to choose a period and filter by prayer/status, or jump directly to a date in the calendar. Week/month summaries keep Prayed, Missed, Not applicable and Unrecorded distinct. Additional sessions remain a separate category.

What happens after a break?

Unlogged prayers stay unrecorded. Returning does not add missed prayers or mark any Prayed. Older records remain editable. A previewed Not applicable day/range preserves Prayed and completed make-up links and never asks for a reason.

Can I use prayer times without location permission?

Yes. Choose a city or enter coordinates and an IANA time zone manually. Use current location is optional foreground access. Select your calculation method and Standard/Hanafi Asr. The timetable shows all five exact clock times and sunrise for a selected date. Remove location clears the saved location. Times can differ from local guidance; update your location after travel.

What do notification taps do?

New alerts say Tap to mark as prayed. A deliberate tap can record the unrecorded original prayer/date once. Receiving or dismissing an alert never logs a prayer. Existing records open for review. A selected city's future date opens the timetable without a future Daily write. Prayer alerts cover up to five days and refresh when Tawba reopens. Choose individual prayers and separate time/check-in switches. The make-up reminder supports a custom time and weekdays.

How do backups and restore work?

Export backup creates an unencrypted file and opens the iOS share sheet. Save it somewhere private outside Tawba. A file-created timestamp does not prove it was saved outside the app. Restore backup previews and validates a file or pasted JSON before replacing, not merging, your records. Export your current records first. Format 7 preserves independent Daily completions and batch history; older formats remain readable. Imported backups cannot enable alerts, saved location, automatic cloud backup or widget details.

Can I undo a restore?

Before replacing readable data, Tawba saves a local checkpoint. Backups & recovery lets you review and restore it; this also checkpoints your current data. Failed replacements preserve previous records. An unreadable original is retained separately during damaged-data recovery. Delete recovery copy removes that original. Local checkpoints do not survive deleting the app or losing the phone.

Is iCloud backup automatic?

Only after you enable it. Backups & recovery offers optional private iCloud backup, an explicit Back up now action and a version list for review, restore and deletion. Automatic snapshots run while Tawba is open after saved changes. Up to ten versions are retained in your private Apple CloudKit database. There is no live cross-device merge. iCloud account, storage, connection and Apple service availability matter; check the last completed backup before changing phones. Turning backup off keeps existing cloud copies until you delete them.

What do widgets and Shortcuts share?

The widget receives a small on-device snapshot of upcoming prayer times. Today's recorded statuses appear only after a separate opt-in. Historical remaining estimates and raw location coordinates are not included. Widget and Shortcut actions open an explicit prayer/date confirmation in Tawba. Opening a link alone does not log anything.

How do I change language?

Choose English or العربية during setup or in Settings > Language. Arabic uses right-to-left presentation, localized dates/numbers and numeric-input support. Prayer times and new notifications use the selected language. User-entered prayer names stay as entered.

How do tips work?

Support Tawba offers one-time Apple tips where available. Apple shows the localized price and asks for confirmation. Tips unlock no features and are not subscriptions or charitable donations. Apple handles payments, purchase history and refund requests. Pending or canceled transactions do not mean a payment completed.

Apple purchase help: https://support.apple.com/billing

What does Delete all data remove?

After confirmation, it removes local records, preferences, checkpoints and recovery copies and cancels reminders. It does not delete files you exported, private iCloud versions, support correspondence or Apple purchase history. Manage cloud versions in Backups & recovery and files at their destination. Sari3 LLC cannot remotely recover local history without a usable backup.
