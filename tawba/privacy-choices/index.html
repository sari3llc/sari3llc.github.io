Your Privacy Choices

Manage prayer records on this device. No Tawba account is required.

Export and restore

Export backup opens the iOS share sheet. Save the unencrypted file somewhere private outside Tawba. Restore previews records and balances before replacing current data; it does not merge histories or infer unrecorded days. A local checkpoint is saved before replacing readable records. Review it in Backups & recovery to undo a restore. Checkpoints disappear if the app is deleted.

Private iCloud backup and widgets

Automatic cloud backup and today’s widget details are separate opt-ins in Backups & recovery. Turning backup off stops new automatic uploads; use the version list to review, restore or delete copies already stored in your private iCloud database. Widget details can be hidden at any time. These preferences are not imported by a backup.

Correct records

Use the calendar to correct Daily statuses, Make-ups and Additional sessions. Settings lets you edit the estimate and daily goal. Unlogged prayers stay unrecorded. Mark a day or date range Not applicable after reviewing the changes; prayed records and completed make-ups are preserved. You do not need to give a reason.

Location and Qibla

Remove location in Settings erases the approximate coordinates and time zone saved for prayer times. Stop the Qibla compass to end its sensor updates. Compass readings are not saved to your prayer history. You can revoke location permission in iPhone Settings. Revoking permission does not itself erase a previously saved prayer-time location.

Notifications

Turn Daily prayer notifications, Not logged yet, and the separate make-up reminder off in Settings, or revoke notification permission in iPhone Settings. Tapping a new prayer alert can mark that unrecorded prayer as Prayed; delivery or dismissal alone does not.

Delete local data

Delete all data asks for confirmation before removing local records, preferences, checkpoints and recovery copies and canceling reminders. It does not delete files you exported or private iCloud versions. Delete cloud versions individually in Backups & recovery. Support correspondence and Apple's purchase history are managed separately.

Tips, email and external copies

Tips are optional one-time Apple purchases, with no subscription or feature unlock. Manage purchase history or refund requests through Apple. Delete exported copies at their chosen destination. Contact sari3@sari3.io about support correspondence; the developer cannot erase or recover your on-device prayer log remotely.
