Your Privacy Choices

Tawba has no account or Sari3 LLC server copy of your prayer history. You control the app’s records directly on your device.

Access and export

Choose Export backup in Tawba Settings to save an unencrypted copy of your prayer records and supported settings through the iOS share sheet. Save it somewhere private outside Tawba before deleting the app or changing phones.

Correct your records

Use the calendar to review or correct daily prayer entries. Use Edit counts to revise the separate remaining estimates for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha. Make-ups reduce only the selected prayer count, and undo restores that same count.

Daily accounting and auto-fill

With Auto-fill daily prayers off, unrecorded closed-day prayers are normally added as missed. Turn Auto-fill on if you prefer completely unrecorded closed-day prayers to be saved as Prayed instead. This setting does not establish whether you actually prayed; explicit records remain authoritative.

Location and Qibla

Remove location in Settings erases the approximate coordinates and time zone saved for prayer times. Stop the Qibla compass to end its sensor updates. Compass readings are not saved to your prayer history. You can also revoke location permission in iPhone Settings.

Notifications

Turn Daily prayer notifications, Not logged yet, and the separate make-up reminder off in Settings, or revoke notification permission in iPhone Settings. Tapping a new prayer alert can mark that unrecorded prayer as Prayed; receiving or dismissing it does not.

Restore or delete local data

Restore backup validates a Tawba file or pasted JSON and previews the result before confirmed replacement. It does not merge histories and has no general undo, so export first. Delete all data in Settings asks for confirmation before removing Tawba’s local records and preferences and canceling reminders and prayer alerts.

Tips, email and external copies

Tips are optional one-time Apple purchases, with no subscription or feature unlock. Manage purchase history or refund requests through Apple. Delete exported copies at their chosen destination. Contact sari3@sari3.io about support correspondence; Sari3 LLC cannot erase or recover your on-device prayer log remotely.
