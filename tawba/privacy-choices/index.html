Your Privacy Choices

Tawba has no account or Sari3 LLC server copy. You control the app’s data directly on your device.

Access and export

Choose Export my data in Tawba Settings to review or save a readable copy of your plan and prayer-log events using the device share sheet.

Correct your data

Change your daily goal or current estimate in Settings. Reverse the most recent prayer entry with Undo last log.

Delete your data

Choose Reset all private data in Settings for immediate local deletion, or delete Tawba from your device. There is no server account to delete.

Control notifications

Turn reminders off in Tawba Settings, or change Tawba’s notification permission in your device settings.

Need help?

Email sari3@sari3.io. Because Tawba does not receive your local records, include only information you are comfortable sharing.
