Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Sari3 LLC handles information in the Tawba mobile application.

Information you enter

Tawba stores your estimated prayer backlog, daily goal, reminder preference, prayer-log events, streak, and progress. New setup uses a starting estimate you enter. It does not ask for time periods, gender, or exemption-day inputs. Plans created in older versions may retain their previous estimate; those versions processed estimator answers temporarily on the device and saved the resulting estimate, not the individual answers. Tawba does not ask for your legal name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, health records, or payment information.

Optional prayer countdown and location

If you enable the next-Salah countdown, Tawba requests foreground location permission with reduced accuracy by default and accesses your location and device time zone. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places before being stored locally, even if you allow precise location in iOS Settings. Tawba uses them only on this device to calculate prayer-time estimates; it does not request background location or send location to Sari3 LLC. Refresh or remove the saved location and time zone in Tawba Settings.

Where your data goes

Your plan and prayer log are processed and stored locally on your device. Tawba has no account system or developer-operated server receiving this information. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup and iCloud settings. Opening a public website or emailing support is separate from the app's local storage; share only information you choose to disclose.

No tracking or advertising

Tawba contains no advertising networks, cross-app tracking, developer analytics, data brokers, or profiling. Sari3 LLC does not sell or rent Tawba information. Tawba does not request tracking permission because it does not track you.

Notifications

If you opt in, iOS stores and delivers a local daily reminder. Tawba uses notification permission only for the reminder you choose. Disable it in Tawba or revoke permission in iOS Settings.

Sharing, export, and manual restore

Tawba shares nothing automatically. Export my data and other share actions open the system share sheet. Information goes to the destination you select and is then governed by that destination's privacy practices. New exports contain your plan and prayer history and omit reminder and location settings. Exports are not encrypted by Tawba.

Manual restore validates the JSON text you paste and asks before replacing local records. It does not upload the backup or merge it with your current history. Restoring turns off reminders and removes the saved countdown location and time zone; re-enable those optional features in Settings. Copies exported by older versions may contain location or reminder information, so review a file before sharing it.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you change it, delete the app, or successfully complete Reset all private data. Reset removes Tawba's saved plan, logs, approximate location, and time zone and cancels its stored reminder. The app reports a failure rather than confirming an incomplete reset. Turning the countdown off removes its saved location and time zone without deleting your plan. Delete copies already exported or retained in a device backup through the destination or Apple's backup controls.

Security

Tawba relies on iOS application sandboxing and device protections. No storage system is infallible, so use a device passcode and keep iOS current. Sari3 LLC cannot remotely recover locally deleted records. Keep any exports private and stored somewhere you trust.

Children

Tawba is a general-audience worship-planning tool. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use where appropriate.

Changes and contact

Material changes will appear here and in the in-app policy with a revised effective date. Contact Sari3 LLC at sari3@sari3.io with privacy questions.
