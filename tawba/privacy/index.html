Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Sari3 LLC handles information in versions of Tawba that display this policy.

Prayer records and settings

Tawba stores your starting estimate, separate remaining counts for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, daily goal, daily prayer statuses, make-up records and links, additional prayers and routines, counting dates, and preferences on your device. Starting estimates are numbers you enter, not calculations of religious obligations or exemptions.

Daily accounting

With Auto-fill daily prayers off, unrecorded prayers after each tracked day closes are added as missed. With it on, completely unrecorded prayers are saved as Prayed instead. Explicit records are preserved. Processing occurs while Tawba is running or when it reopens; it is not evidence that a prayer was performed. You can correct entries in the calendar.

Prayer-time location

After you choose Use current location, Tawba requests foreground location permission. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places and saved with your time zone on this device for prayer-time calculations. They are not sent to Sari3 LLC. Remove location in Settings deletes these saved values. Revoking permission alone does not erase a location already saved.

Qibla compass

Start compass uses foreground location and device-heading readings to estimate the Qibla direction. These readings are temporary, are not added to your prayer records or backups, and are not sent to Sari3 LLC. Sensor updates stop when you stop the compass, leave its screen, or background the app. Tawba does not request background location.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and scheduled locally by iOS. Prayer alerts use your saved location, calculation settings and daily log, cover up to five days ahead, and refresh when Tawba reopens. New prayer alerts say “Tap to mark as prayed”; tapping records the original prayer and date only if unrecorded. Receiving or dismissing an alert does not log a prayer. Prayer names may appear on your lock screen.

Local storage and recovery

There is no Tawba account, developer-operated prayer database, automatic cloud backup or cross-device sync. Do not assume iPhone or iCloud backups include your prayer records. Use Export backup and save a copy outside Tawba before deleting the app or changing phones. Sari3 LLC cannot remotely recover lost local records. Tawba relies on iOS device protections and the app sandbox for local storage.

Exports and restore

Export backup creates an unencrypted file and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose its destination; Tawba does not upload it automatically. New backups preserve prayer records, per-prayer remaining counts, routines, counting dates and auto-fill. Restore validates a selected file or pasted JSON and asks before replacing, not merging, records. If tracked days have elapsed, you choose how to handle unrecorded prayers and see the resulting total. Export your current data first; there is no general undo-restore feature.

Optional Apple tips

Where available, one-time developer-support tips use Apple’s in-app purchase system. Tawba requests available products and localized prices from Apple. Apple handles billing and purchase history. Tips unlock no features, are not subscriptions, and are not charitable fundraising. Tawba does not receive your payment-card details.

Apple services and external links

Apple separately handles App Store distribution, purchases and diagnostics under its own terms. TestFlight provides beta usage and crash information to Apple and the developer. Opening a support or policy website, composing email, or sharing an export involves the selected browser, mail or destination service and its privacy practices.

Support messages

If you email support, you choose to share your sender address, message and any attachments with Sari3 LLC and its email service. These can be used to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details or private prayer-history files. Contact support to request deletion of correspondence; necessary legal or security retention may apply. Tawba does not automatically attach your prayer log.

Retention and deletion

Your local records remain until you change or delete them. Delete all data in Settings asks for confirmation, cancels Tawba reminders and prayer alerts, and removes its saved records, settings, onboarding draft and retained recovery copy. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Neither action deletes files you exported, support emails or Apple’s purchase history.

No advertising or tracking

Tawba has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, does not sell prayer records, and does not use cross-app tracking or data brokers. The developer does not receive your prayer records through ordinary app use.

Children and contact

Tawba is a general-audience planning tool and does not require children to create accounts. Please avoid sending a child’s personal information to support. Material changes will be reflected in this policy. For privacy questions, contact Sari3 LLC at sari3@sari3.io.
