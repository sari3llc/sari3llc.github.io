Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Sari3 LLC handles information in the Tawba mobile application.

Information you enter

Tawba stores your estimated prayer backlog, daily goal, exemption-day inputs, reminder preference, prayer-log events, streak, and progress. It does not ask for your legal name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, health records, or payment information.

Where your data goes

Your plan and prayer log are processed and stored locally on your device. Tawba has no account system or Sari3 LLC server receiving this information. Device backups may include app data according to your device backup and cloud settings.

No tracking or advertising

Tawba contains no advertising networks, cross-app tracking, developer analytics, data brokers, or profiling. Sari3 LLC does not sell or rent Tawba information. Tawba does not request tracking permission because it does not track you.

Notifications

If you opt in, your device stores and delivers a local daily reminder. Tawba uses notification permission only for the reminder you choose. You may disable it in Tawba or revoke permission in device settings.

Sharing and export

Tawba shares nothing automatically. If you choose Export my data or another share action, the operating system presents its share sheet. Information goes only to the destination you select and is then governed by that destination’s privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you change it, delete Tawba, or choose Reset all private data. Reset removes Tawba’s saved plan and logs and cancels its stored reminder. Delete exported copies or backup copies through the destination or device backup controls.

Security

Tawba relies on operating-system application sandboxing and device protections. No storage system is infallible, so use a device passcode and keep your device current. Sari3 LLC cannot recover locally deleted records.

Children

Tawba is a general-audience worship-planning tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. A parent or guardian should supervise a child’s use where appropriate.

Changes and contact

Material changes will appear here and in the in-app policy with a revised effective date. Contact Sari3 LLC at sari3@sari3.io with privacy questions.
