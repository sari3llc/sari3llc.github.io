Privacy Policy

Applies to versions of Tawba that display this policy.

Prayer records and settings

Tawba stores your starting estimate, remaining balance, daily goal, daily prayer statuses, make-up records and links, additional prayers and routines, counting dates, and preferences on your device. Older records may include a name you previously entered. New setup uses your own starting number, not a calculation of religious obligations or exemptions. Not-applicable entries do not require a reason.

Daily accounting

Unlogged prayers stay unrecorded after midnight, time away and backup restoration. Tawba does not infer whether you prayed. Existing records and balances from older versions stay intact until you correct them. You choose whether a recorded missed prayer is added to your estimate or already included. Not applicable can be applied to a day or date range after reviewing its effects; prayed records and completed make-ups are preserved. No reason for choosing Not applicable is requested or stored.

Prayer-time location

After you choose Use current location, Tawba requests foreground location permission. Reduced accuracy is requested by default. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places and saved with your time zone on this device for prayer-time calculations. They are not sent to Sari3 LLC. Remove location in Settings deletes these saved values. Revoking permission alone does not erase a location already saved.

Qibla compass

Start compass uses foreground location and device-heading readings to estimate the direction of the Qibla. These readings are used temporarily, are not added to saved prayer records or backups, and are not sent to Sari3 LLC. Sensor updates stop when you stop the compass, leave its screen, or background the app. Tawba does not request background location. Permission and reading quality can affect availability and accuracy.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and scheduled locally by iOS. Prayer alerts use your saved location, calculation settings and daily log, cover up to five days ahead, and refresh when Tawba reopens. New prayer alerts say Tap to mark as prayed: tapping records the original prayer and date only if unrecorded. Existing records are not overwritten. Receiving or dismissing an alert does not log a prayer. Older alerts open the calendar only. Prayer names may appear on your lock screen. Disable alerts in Tawba or revoke permission in iPhone Settings.

Local storage and recovery

There is no Tawba account, developer-operated prayer database, automatic cloud backup or cross-device sync. Do not assume iPhone or iCloud backups include your prayer records. Use Export backup and save a copy outside Tawba before deleting the app or changing phones. Sari3 LLC cannot remotely recover lost local records. Local storage has no additional encryption supplied by Tawba; it relies on iOS device protections and the app sandbox.

Exports and restore

Export backup creates an unencrypted file and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose its destination; the app does not upload it automatically. Exports contain your plan, prayer records, routines and legacy counting metadata, not notification or saved-location settings. Restore validates a selected file or pasted JSON and asks before replacing, not merging, records. Unlogged days stay unrecorded; an older backup cannot restart automatic counting or auto-fill. Restoring keeps alerts, reminders and saved location off. Older backups may omit newer records; review the preview and export your current data first. There is no general undo-restore feature.

Optional Apple tips

Where available, one-time developer-support tips use Apple's in-app purchase system. Tawba requests available products and localized prices from Apple. A purchase requires a tip selection and Apple's confirmation. Apple handles billing and purchase history. Tawba checks transaction results on the device; product and transaction identifiers are handled in memory, not included in prayer backups or sent to a Sari3 payment server. Apple may provide sales and financial reports to the developer. Tips unlock no features and are not subscriptions or charitable fundraising.

Apple services and external links

Apple separately handles App Store distribution, purchases and diagnostics under its own terms. TestFlight provides beta usage and crash information to Apple and the developer. App Store diagnostics may also be shared according to your device settings. This is separate from Tawba uploading its prayer database. Opening a support or policy website, composing email, or sharing an export involves the selected browser, mail or destination service and its privacy practices.

Support messages

If you email support, you choose to share your sender address, message and any attachments with Sari3 LLC and its email service. These can be used to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details or private prayer-history files. Contact support to request deletion of correspondence; necessary legal or security retention may apply. Tawba does not automatically attach your prayer log.

Retention and deletion

Your local records remain until you change or delete them. Delete all data in Settings asks for confirmation, cancels Tawba reminders and prayer alerts, and removes its saved records, settings, onboarding draft and retained recovery copy. Recovery from unreadable storage can retain the original bytes locally; Delete recovery copy removes that copy separately. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Neither action deletes files you exported, support emails or Apple's purchase history; manage those with the destination or contact the relevant service.

No advertising or tracking

Tawba has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, does not sell prayer records, and does not use cross-app tracking or data brokers. The developer does not receive your prayer records through ordinary app use.

Children and contact

Tawba is a general-audience planning tool, not a service that requires children to create accounts. Please avoid sending a child's personal information to support. Material changes will be reflected in this policy. For privacy questions, contact Sari3 LLC at sari3@sari3.io.
